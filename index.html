What the price difference means

Takeover covers the hotel, food, parties and bus. Lift pass and rental are extra. With us the hotel is approx. €70, so the extra (approx. €120 per person) is yours to spend on the actual ski pass and stuff. 19–22 March also gives you 2 full days to ski.

Ski day plan

07:30

Before 12:30
Pick up your ski or snowboard gear and boots.
12:30
Head up to the resort. This is when the student half-day pass becomes valid.
~16:00
Mountain lifts close.
17:00
Gondola closes, so the last ride down.
18:00
Whole place is closed. Return gear to the shop.
18:30–20:30
Next-day fittings are available at the rental shop.
